TECHNICAL DEEP DIVE: BEVERAGE & PLANT PROTEIN

Overcoming Thermal Shock & Phase Separation in RTD Beverages

The Challenge

Commercial UHT processing (140°C for 4 seconds) often destabilizes plant-protein beverages, causing insoluble sedimentation and phase separation within 72 hours of bottling under ambient retail conditions.

PureBlend Formulation Approach

A co-processed synergy of PureBlend Xanthan Gum 200 Mesh (0.08% w/w) and Pectin Citrus HM (0.14% w/w) creates a pseudoplastic protective colloid matrix that suspends insoluble proteins without increasing perceived viscosity or mouth-coating.
