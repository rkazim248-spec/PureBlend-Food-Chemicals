Scientific Metallurgy & Organic Synthesis

Product Overview & Reaction Kinetics

Citric acid (2-hydroxypropane-1,2,3-tricarboxylic acid) anhydrous is isolated via advanced multiple-stage crystallization from submerged aerobic bio-fermentation broth.

Key Thermodynamic Parameters:
Solubility in Water (20°C): 59.2% (w/w)
Apparent Dissociation pKa₁: 3.13 at 25°C
Apparent Dissociation pKa₂: 4.76 at 25°C
Apparent Dissociation pKa₃: 6.40 at 25°C
Melting Temperature: ~153°C (anhydrous)

Unlike monohydrate citric acid, PureBlend Bio-Acidulant Elite Anhydrous exhibits negligible equilibrium moisture migration below 65% relative humidity. This thermodynamic stability eliminates agglomeration and compaction during bulk pneumatic conveying, dry blending, and high-speed multi-head weighing operations.

Beyond straightforward pH attenuation, the tri-carboxylic structure forms tight coordination complexes with polyvalent trace metal ions (primarily Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺, and Cu²⁺). By sequestering pro-oxidant transition metals, it functions as a highly synergistic oxidation retardant when paired with ascorbic acid or tocopherols, preventing lipid rancidity in emulsion matrices without requiring synthetic EDTA.
