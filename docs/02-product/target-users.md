# Target Users

## Public Visitors
- Food technologists / procurement managers evaluating ingredients (primary business audience)
- Quality/R&D personnel researching specifications
- General visitors seeking company information
- Requirements: clear specs, trustworthy content, fast answers

## Admin Users
- PureBlend marketing/content staff managing products, banners, offers, FAQs, SEO
- Requirements: simple CRUD, clear feedback, safe publish/unpublish

## Jury/Evaluators (hackathon audience)
- Expect a working, polished demo covering all organizer criteria
