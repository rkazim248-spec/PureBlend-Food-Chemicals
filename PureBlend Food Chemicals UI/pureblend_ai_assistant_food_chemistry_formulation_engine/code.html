What is the difference between PureBlend Citric Acid Anhydrous and Monohydrate in shelf-stable beverage formulation?

SC
biotech
PureBlend AI • Chemical Formulation Engine
verified Verified Internal Dossier Grounding

In shelf-stable ready-to-drink (RTD) beverage formulation, the critical distinction between PureBlend Citric Acid Anhydrous and PureBlend Citric Acid Monohydrate centers on water of crystallization, molecular potency, hydration thermodynamics, and hygroscopic stability in batch staging.

water_drop Anhydrous Grade (USP / FCC)

Contains no molecular bound water (<0.5% KF assay). Offers 8.8% greater active titratable acidity per kilogram compared to monohydrate, enabling lower volumetric dosing. Ideal for liquid concentrates, flavor syrups, and dry beverage premixes where moisture migration causes premature caking.

science Monohydrate Grade (USP / FCC)

Contains one water molecule per citric molecule (7.5% – 9.0% bound moisture). Exhibits an endothermic heat of solution and distinct crystalline cleavage, dissolving smoothly in ambient blending tanks without rapid thermal shock. Favored in traditional hot-fill canning facilities.
