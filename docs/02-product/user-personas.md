# User Personas

## Persona 1 — Procurement Buyer (Visitor)
- Needs: quick product specs, offers, contact route
- Pain: outdated websites, missing info
- Success: finds a product and submits an inquiry in minutes

## Persona 2 — R&D / Quality Specialist (Visitor)
- Needs: accurate technical details, FAQs
- Success: chatbot answers a spec question from approved knowledge

## Persona 3 — Content Admin (Admin)
- Needs: fast CRUD, publish/unpublish, no developer help
- Success: updates a banner and sees it live on the homepage within one refresh

## Persona 4 — Technical Director (Admin)
- Needs: confidence that nothing breaks, SEO managed centrally
- Success: sets meta titles for products and sees them in page source
