# Product Vision

PureBlend's web presence should feel like a modern, trustworthy B2B/B2C food-ingredient company: precise, premium, and clear. The site should make it effortless for a buyer to discover products, understand offers, get answers, and contact the team — while giving staff full control over every piece of content through the admin dashboard. The AI chatbot acts as a knowledgeable first point of contact, strictly grounded in PureBlend's approved information.

## Pillars
1. **Trust** — professional design, accurate info, secure experience
2. **Clarity** — clean information architecture, scannable content
3. **Control** — admin can manage everything without developers
4. **Intelligence** — RAG chatbot that stays on-topic and honest about limits
5. **Performance** — fast, lightweight, mobile-first
